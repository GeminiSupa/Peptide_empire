# Business accounts rollout

1. Run `business-accounts.sql` in the same Supabase project after `customer-accounts-foundation.sql`.
2. Run `business-accounts-suite.sql` for team buyers, product prices, lists, quotes, referral codes, email retries, and order snapshots. The migration also reserves each existing company's owner mailbox in its membership table. Run it before deploying the expanded business tools.
3. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_ACCOUNTS_LIVE=true` and rebuild to make customer sign-in and dashboards publicly accessible. Existing account preview remains available for staging.
4. Verify transactional `ORDER_SMTP_*` settings and the canonical `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`. Also configure `OWN_DOMAIN_SMTP_*` for delivery to the company's Rackspace inbox. The existing request-code flow verifies mailbox ownership; welcome and order emails reuse the existing customer flow.
5. As the superadmin, open Customers → Customer accounts → Business accounts. Enter the company, dedicated buyer email, 10-digit cédula jurídica, contact, addresses, percentage, minimum order rules, assigned rep, and agreed commission. Saving a new account or changed status/pricing queues a customer notice automatically. **Send invitation** sends a separate branded welcome.
6. The buyer signs in using the existing email-code flow and opens `/account/business`. Orders, delivery tracking, addresses and support remain in the customer account area.
7. New buyers may apply at `/account/business` after signing in. Applications remain pending with zero discount until approved. They receive a branded application receipt; delivery failures are surfaced without losing the application. Send the invitation after approval to confirm the terms.

Pricing applies to non-BAC merchandise and excludes shipping. The best total of business pricing and existing public offers wins; business terms never stack with another offer. Both catalog checkout and order creation use the same pricing engine. Suspended accounts lose business pricing on subsequent orders, while retaining ordinary customer account access.

Company identity and pricing changes require staff review. Each company has one primary owner email and can invite separate manager and buyer logins. Corporation numbers are format-checked, not verified against a government registry. Billing statements are printable commercial order records; official electronic tax invoices require a separate integration.

Before launch: create a test business with a mailbox you control; confirm invitation delivery, sign-in code delivery, dashboard access, both currencies, a better public offer, and suspension. Check that a second customer cannot access the company. No production invitations are sent by the implementation task.

## Signup alerts

New business registrations, whether submitted by the buyer or created by the owner, send separate alerts to `omerforce@gmail.com` and `info@peptidescostarica.net`. The info copy uses own-domain SMTP; the Omer copy uses transactional SMTP. Customer acknowledgements are separate messages. An unsuccessful staff alert does not discard the business account or the other successful delivery. The owner can use **Resend staff alert** in Business accounts after correcting SMTP configuration. SMTP acceptance is not proof that a message reached the inbox.

## Passwords and recovery

Email-code sign-in remains available for first-time registration. After verifying their email, a customer can set a password under **You → Login & security** (`/account/security`). Password login is available on the sign-in screen. **Forgot password** verifies an email code and then opens the password settings page; recovery does not depend on a pre-existing password. Passwords must match and contain 12–128 characters. A recent sign-in is checked before updates; older sessions are directed back to email verification. Successful password updates attempt to revoke other device sessions and report any failure separately. Supabase remains the password authority; credentials are never stored in business profiles or emailed to staff.

## Expanded business tools

- **Product prices:** Edit a company, then use Business operations to set USD prices for exact catalog products. Prices are converted using the current checkout exchange rate. Each line receives the better of its agreed product price or company percentage; public offers can still win on the total. Rates never stack. BAC-water prices are excluded.
- **Team buyers:** The company owner can invite managers and buyers. Managers can add/remove ordinary buyers. Buyers cannot manage memberships. Every invited mailbox must sign in and verify itself. Members share company pricing, lists, quotes, and company order history. Removing membership immediately removes company pricing/access on the next server request; their ordinary customer account remains.
- **Quick orders and saved lists:** `/account/business/tools` provides a searchable quantity grid and shared saved lists. Existing storefront caps remain: 50 product lines and 100 units per line. Stock and prices are rechecked by checkout. Ordinary reorders merge with the cart; quote orders explicitly replace it.
- **Quotes:** Approved businesses request merchandise quantities. The owner prices the exact requested lines and sets an expiry within 90 days. The requester receives an email when reviewed. Checkout verifies company, approval, expiry, and exact product quantities. Better current account/public pricing can still apply. A database unique index permits only one order per quote, including simultaneous requests.
- **Billing downloads:** Company buyers can download printable HTML statements for their company orders. Legal name, corporation number and billing address come from the immutable order snapshot. These records do not assert payment or substitute for an electronic tax invoice.
- **Rep attribution and commissions:** Referral codes resolve to active team members on the server. Company orders retain the assigned rep and agreed commission as an order snapshot. Business attribution replaces competing affiliate payouts. A zero commission stays zero rather than reverting to the rep's default. Reports use existing paid/refund/payout rules, with historical rep/rate data preserved. Admin order edits retain the original product-price/percentage agreement unless staff explicitly replace it with a manual discount.
- **Brochure QR signup:** Create a code in Business operations, show/download its QR, and print it on that rep's brochures. Signup preserves the code across email verification. Printed codes cannot be reassigned to another rep; they can be disabled. Changing the browser code cannot invent a rep, commission, approval, or discount.
- **Automatic notices:** Account creation, approval/suspension, percentage/minimum changes, product-price changes, member invitations, and quote reviews queue customer emails. Jobs are claimed atomically and failed/stale jobs can be retried from Business operations. These are transactional messages, not campaigns.
- **Minimum orders:** Staff can set minimum non-BAC unit counts and a minimum products total in USD after discounts, excluding shipping. The server enforces both for approved company orders.
- **Sales reporting:** Business operations shows approved accounts, paid order count, net sales after refunds, repeat buyers, last purchase, 30-day follow-up flags, and rep commissions. Reporting explicitly flags its 20,000-order limit. Company order history and quote lists currently show the latest 200 and 100 records respectively.

The automated tests use isolated account/database/mail adapters; they do not create live customers, send production mail, or apply migrations. Production build and automated validation are separate from live database and inbox verification. No live dashboard or deployment changes are part of this expansion.
