Our Story

We started this because we were tired of hearing “it's stuck in customs.”

Peptides Costa Rica was founded by Joey Webster and Sean McCully, two training partners from California who made Costa Rica home and saw how hard it was for local labs to get documented research compounds without weeks of waiting.

Chapter 1

Built in the gym.

Joey and Sean met the way a lot of real friendships start: on the mats. Combat sports don't reward talk. They reward showing up, doing the work and owning your mistakes.

Years of training together turned into a friendship built on discipline, commitment and respect. When they started a business, they didn't write values on a wall. They just ran it the way they trained.

Chapter 2

The problem nobody was fixing.

Friends from the gym worked at a certified research laboratory in Costa Rica. Their projects kept stalling for the same reason: the compounds they needed, peptides included, had to come from international suppliers.

Orders sat in customs. Import fees showed up unannounced. Nobody could say how the vials had been stored on the way. And when something went wrong, there was no one to call.

Joey and Sean saw a gap: a local supplier that kept stock here, tested its products and actually picked up the phone.

Chapter 3

Learning it properly.

Before selling a single vial, they learned the field: peptide specifications, purity testing, what a Certificate of Analysis should contain, how lyophilised material must be stored and handled.

Then they built the company around what they wished existed: local stock, batch documentation, transparent per-vial pricing and direct order confirmation before payment.

Today

A local business, for Costa Rica only.

We serve customers in Costa Rica and nowhere else. That focus is the point: it lets us confirm stock before you pay, coordinate delivery directly, and stand behind every order personally.

We don't provide dosing, administration instructions or treatment recommendations. Our products are supplied for laboratory research, and our job is to make sure the material you receive is exactly what the label and the COA say it is.
