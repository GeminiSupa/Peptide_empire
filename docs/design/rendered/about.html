Why we exist

Ordering research peptides from abroad is a gamble. We took the gamble out.

Before Peptides Costa Rica, a lab or researcher here had two options: wait weeks for an international package that might get held at customs, or buy from a local reseller with no documentation and no idea how the vials were stored. We built a third option.
