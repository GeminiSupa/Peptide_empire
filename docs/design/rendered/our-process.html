The chain of custody

From qualified source to your lab bench.

Each step exists because something can go wrong there. Here's how we close the gap.

  1. 1
    verified_user

    Qualified sources

    Before a manufacturing partner ships us a single lot, they pass our qualification. We look at their production standards, their documentation and their track record. Partners only stay on our list while their batches keep passing.

    Why it matters: consistency starts before the material exists.
  2. 2
    science

    Independent testing

    Most products are sent to an independent third-party laboratory for identity and purity analysis before they're offered for sale.

    Why it matters: the label is a claim; the test is the proof.
  3. 3
    lab_profile

    Published documentation

    Certificates of Analysis are published in our COA database. Your vial carries a batch number; the report carries the same number.

    Why it matters: you can verify the exact lot you receive.
  4. 4
    fact_check

    Receiving inspection

    When a lot arrives, we check it by hand: seals intact, labels matching the order, batch numbers matching the documentation, quantities matching the invoice.

    Why it matters: errors get caught here, not in your lab.
  5. 5
    ac_unit

    Cold storage on site

    Lyophilised vials go straight into our own refrigerated storage at 2–8 °C, protected from light, and stay there until the day they ship. We don't use third-party warehouses.

    Why it matters: peptide stability depends on how material is kept.
  6. 6
    task_alt

    Confirmed before you pay

    When you order, we confirm the exact stock and send the full total before any payment is taken. No backorders, no substitutions without your approval.

    Why it matters: you never pay for something we can't send.
  7. 7
    package_2

    Sealed, secure packaging

    Every order, from one vial to fifty, is sealed and packed securely for transport across Costa Rica's roads and climate.

    Why it matters: material arrives the way it left.
  8. 8
    local_shipping

    Tracked local delivery

    Orders are processed in 1–2 business days and delivered in 1–2 business days after dispatch, with tracking. No customs, no import fees, no border.

    Why it matters: fewer days in transit, fewer things that can go wrong.
  9. 9
    support_agent

    Support after delivery

    If a vial arrives damaged or doesn't match your order, message us within 7 days with photos. We replace it.

    Why it matters: our responsibility doesn't end at your door.
