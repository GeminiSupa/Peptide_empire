How to add it to your home screen.

The app is a web app (a "PWA"): it opens from your browser and installs like a normal app, with its own icon. Pick your phone:

  1. app.peptidescostarica.net
    1. Open the app in Safari

    Tap the button below on your iPhone. It must be Safari: other iPhone browsers can't install apps.

  2. CopyAdd to Reading ListAdd to Home Screen Find on Page
    2. Tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”

    Share is the square with an arrow, at the bottom of Safari. Scroll the menu down if you don't see the option.

  3. 3. Tap “Add”. Done.

    The Peptides CR icon appears on your home screen. Open it like any app. Turn on notifications to hear about restocks.
