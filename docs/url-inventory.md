## The Spanish URLs are not `/es/` + the English path

This is the single most important finding in this document, and the previous
data-model note got it wrong.

TranslatePress is running with **translated slugs**. `/about/` is served at
`/es/acerca-de/` — `/es/about/` 301s to it. So Spanish routing cannot be
derived from a `localePath(path, 'es')` helper; it is per-page data.

```
/about/                    → /es/acerca-de/
/bulk-discounts/           → /es/descuentos-por-volumen/
/lab-tests-coa-results/    → /es/resultados-de-pruebas-de-laboratorio-coa/
/product/bpc-157-10mg/     → /es/producto-2/bpc-157-10mg/
/product-category/…/       → /es/categoria-producto-2/…/
```

Note `producto-2` and `categoria-producto-2` — the `-2` is a WordPress slug
collision that is now permanent, indexed SEO. It has to be reproduced exactly.

**131 of 321 Spanish slugs are identical to their English slug** (88 city pages,
22 product landers, 16 guides). There is no rule for which get translated. Both
cases are live URLs and both must be preserved verbatim.

Routing therefore resolves through `data/routes.json`, not through a path
transform. `lib/routes.js` does this: content stores the English canonical path
for every internal link and `localePath()` looks up the Spanish twin.

(An earlier version of this note warned that `lib/site.js` exported a
`/es{path}` helper that had to be replaced. No such file exists — the lookup was
already implemented correctly in `lib/routes.js`.)

## Structural notes for the rebuild

- **Two parallel product namespaces.** `/bpc-157-costa-rica/` (Elementor
  marketing lander, 40 of them) and `/product/bpc-157-10mg/` (WooCommerce
  object, 67 of them). Both are indexed. The relationship is many-to-one:
  `/ghk-cu-costa-rica/` covers both `/product/ghk-cu-50mg/` and
  `/product/ghk-cu-100mg/`.
- **Two parallel category namespaces**, likewise: `/peptides-for-skin/` (page,
  14 of them) and `/product-category/peptides-for-skin/` (archive, 15 of them).
  Verified by rendering: the marketing category page carries a Woo product grid
  (25 products on `/peptides-for-weight-loss/`) and almost no prose; the guides
  carry ~3,000 words and no products.
- **Two singular slugs**, easy to typo into a 404:
  `/peptide-for-cognitive-function/` and
  `/peptide-for-cardiovascular-costa-rica/`.
- **The city pages are the largest single group (93)** and the guides are second
  (56). Together that is 149 of 322 English URLs — 46% of the site, and none of
  it was in the original brief.
